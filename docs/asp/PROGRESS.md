# FACE ASP — progress, decisions and open questions

FACE ASP is the cohort of Asperger syndrome and autism spectrum disorders (ASD),
children and adults.

Extraction: `ASPALL_20261030.tsv` (4,500 rows × 4,364 columns, 2,318 patients). See D1.
Dictionary: `thesauri/FACETSASDI_thesaurus_V2.13.xlsx` (not in git), received on
2026-11-14 after the module was written. The schemas were written without it, from
the source tables of the export (D2); it was then compared with them tab by tab
(D13) and applied to them (D14).

The architecture and the schema fields are those of FACE BP, SZ and DR
([ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md), [SCHEMA_SPEC.md](../SCHEMA_SPEC.md)). The
decisions of [the BP file](../bp/PROGRESS.md), [the SZ file](../sz/PROGRESS.md) and
[the DR file](../dr/PROGRESS.md) are quoted as "BP D9", "SZ D12" and "DR D9".
