# FACE DR — progress, decisions and open questions

FACE DR is the cohort of treatment-resistant depression (TRD).

Extraction: `DRALL_20261030.tsv` (2,193 rows × 2,375 columns, 634 patients). See D1.
Dictionary: `FACEDR_thesaurus_V4.16.xlsx`.

The architecture and the schema fields are those of FACE BP and FACE SZ
([ARCHITECTURE.md](../ARCHITECTURE.md), [SCHEMA_SPEC.md](../SCHEMA_SPEC.md)). The
decisions of [the BP file](../bp/PROGRESS.md) and of [the SZ file](../sz/PROGRESS.md)
are quoted as "BP D9" and "SZ D12".
